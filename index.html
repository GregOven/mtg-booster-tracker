How this works
  • Click a set's header to expand or collapse it. Check off each pack you own.
  • Fill in Market price on a pack — what it costs to buy today. Once you own the pack the same field becomes Value now, and Collection value adds those up (packs without it count as 0). A price of 0 on a pack you don't own flags it in red as currently unbuyable.
  • Fill in a set's Release date to unlock its age badge, chronological pack numbering, and the "Packs by year" chart.
  • The i bubble next to some set names opens a bit of Magic history trivia about that set.
  • Click the ☆ next to a pack to mark it as something you're planning to buy, and paste a link to where you found it. Choose To buy in the filter menu to see just that list.
  • Sets you added yourself have a ✏ button to edit them (rename, change packs, milestone) and a 🗑 button to delete them.
  • Use Export CSV for a spreadsheet-friendly copy, or Backup (JSON) for a full, restorable backup of everything (including your plan-to-buy list and custom sets).
